Gym Program

Building strength and power for 400m/800m excellence

Current Program

Recommended Programs for 400m/800m

🏗️ General Preparation (Oct-Dec)

Build strength foundation, muscle mass, work capacity. Focus on compound movements with moderate-high volume.

Day 1: Lower Body Strength

  • Back Squat: 4x6-8 reps @ 75-80% 1RM
  • Romanian Deadlift: 3x8-10
  • Bulgarian Split Squat: 3x8 each leg
  • Leg Curl: 3x10-12
  • Calf Raises: 3x15
  • Core: Plank variations 3x45s

Day 2: Upper Body + Power

  • Bench Press: 4x6-8
  • Bent-Over Row: 3x8-10
  • Overhead Press: 3x8
  • Pull-ups: 3x max reps
  • Medicine Ball Slams: 3x10 (explosive)
  • Core: Anti-rotation press 3x10 each side

⚡ Specific Preparation (Jan-Mar)

Transition to power and speed-strength. Lower volume, higher intensity, more explosive movements. Key phase for 400m/800m athletes.

Day 1: Power & Acceleration

  • Power Clean: 5x3 @ 80-85% 1RM (EXPLOSIVE)
  • Box Jumps: 4x5 (max height, full recovery)
  • Trap Bar Deadlift: 4x4 @ 85%
  • Depth Jumps: 3x5
  • Single-Leg Bounds: 3x8 each leg
  • Core: Med ball rotational throws 3x8

Day 2: Speed-Strength Lower

  • Jump Squat: 5x5 @ 30-40% bodyweight (MAX SPEED)
  • Single-Leg Squat: 3x6 each leg
  • Nordic Curls: 3x6-8
  • Lateral Bounds: 3x10 each direction
  • Heavy Sled Push: 4x20m
  • Core: Ab wheel rollouts 3x10

🏆 Competition Phase (Apr-Sep)

Maintenance only. Keep strength, avoid fatigue. 1-2 sessions per week, low volume.

Maintenance Session (once or twice weekly)

  • Back Squat or Trap Bar Deadlift: 3x4 @ 80%
  • Power Clean: 3x3 @ 75%
  • Box Jumps: 3x4
  • Single-Leg Work: 2x6 each leg
  • Core: 2-3 exercises x 8-10 reps

⚠️ Keep intensity high, volume LOW. Should feel fresh, not fatigued.

Exercise Library - 400m/800m Specific

Key Compound Lifts

Back Squat

Why: Foundation for leg strength, force production

Focus: Depth, controlled eccentric, explosive concentric

Power Clean

Why: Triple extension power (ankle, knee, hip) - mimics sprint mechanics

Focus: Speed of bar, full hip extension

Trap Bar Deadlift

Why: Posterior chain power, safer than conventional deadlift

Focus: Explosive hip drive

Romanian Deadlift

Why: Hamstring strength (crucial for 400m/800m finish)

Focus: Eccentric control, hamstring stretch

Plyometrics & Power

Box Jumps

Why: Reactive strength, vertical power

Focus: Max height, soft landing, full recovery between reps

Depth Jumps

Why: Reactive strength, rapid force development

Focus: Minimal ground contact time

Bounds (Single & Double Leg)

Why: Horizontal power (directly transfers to sprinting)

Focus: Distance per bound, aggressive arm drive

Medicine Ball Throws

Why: Rotational power, core explosiveness

Focus: Maximum velocity of throw

Unilateral (Single-Leg)

Bulgarian Split Squat

Why: Address strength imbalances, knee stability

Focus: Front leg driving force

Single-Leg RDL

Why: Balance, hamstring strength, hip stability

Focus: Maintain level hips

Step-Ups

Why: Knee drive power (late acceleration phase)

Focus: Explosive drive off front foot

Injury Prevention & Prehab

Nordic Curls

Why: Eccentric hamstring strength (prevent strains)

Critical: Essential for 400m athletes

Copenhagen Planks

Why: Adductor strength (groin injury prevention)

Focus: Hold 20-30s each side

Calf Raises (Straight & Bent Knee)

Why: Achilles health, ankle stiffness

Focus: Full range of motion, both variations
