Fuel for world-class performance

Guidelines for 400m/800m Athletes

🏃 Hard Training Days

  • Carbs: 6-8g per kg bodyweight
  • Protein: 1.6-2.0g per kg
  • Timing: Carbs 2-3hrs pre-session
  • Recovery: 20-30g protein within 30min

🧘 Recovery Days

  • Carbs: 3-5g per kg bodyweight
  • Protein: 1.6-2.0g per kg
  • Focus: Anti-inflammatory foods
  • Hydration: 2-3L water minimum

🏆 Competition Days

  • 3-4 hrs before: High-carb meal (familiar foods)
  • 1-2 hrs before: Light carb snack
  • 30min before: Simple carbs if needed (banana, gel)
  • After: Recovery meal within 2 hours
