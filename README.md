# Journey to 2032 - Elite Athletics Training Platform

A comprehensive training and development platform designed to help you progress from current performance to world-class level by 2032.

## Features

### 🏃 Training Hub
- **Session Logging** - Track every workout with detailed notes, quality ratings, and technical focus points
- **Accountability System** - Visual progress bars showing daily training consistency leading to competitions
- **Streak Tracking** - Monitor consecutive training days to build consistency
- **Whoop Integration** - Log recovery and strain data alongside sessions

### 🎯 Goals & Progress
- **Competition Timeline** - Add upcoming meets and visualize countdown
- **Progress Tracking** - Track progress toward 45.XX (400m) and sub-1:46 (800m)
- **Personal Bests** - Record and track improvements across events
- **Accountability Bars** - Green/red progress bars showing training consistency to each competition

### 🥗 Nutrition
- **Tesco-Friendly Meal Plans** - Budget-conscious meal suggestions for training, recovery, and competition days
- **Hydration Tracking** - Daily water intake monitoring
- **Shopping List Generator** - Automated weekly shopping lists
- **Meal Planning** - Training-based meal recommendations

### 🏋️ Gym Program
- **Phase-Based Programs** - Detailed programs for General Prep, Specific Prep, and Competition phases
- **Exercise Library** - 400m/800m specific exercises with coaching points
- **Program Tracking** - Log current phase and sessions per week

### 📊 Analytics
- **Session Quality Analysis** - Track quality distribution and trends
- **Training Volume** - Monitor weekly breakdown and balance
- **Technical Focus Tracking** - See how often you hit technical checkpoints
- **Insights** - AI-generated recommendations based on your data

### 🌟 Portfolio (Sponsorship Ready)
- **Professional Presentation** - Clean, sponsor-ready showcase
- **Career Highlights** - Timeline of achievements
- **Competition Results** - Performance history
- **About & Philosophy** - Editable sections to share your story
- **Contact Information** - Easy way for sponsors to reach you

## Setup Instructions

### 1. Upload to GitHub

1. Create a new repository on GitHub
2. Upload all files from this platform to your repository
3. Make sure the repository is public

### 2. Enable GitHub Pages

1. Go to your repository settings
2. Navigate to "Pages" in the left sidebar
3. Under "Source", select "main" branch
4. Click Save
5. Your site will be published at: `https://yourusername.github.io/repository-name`

### 3. Customize Your Profile

1. Open `portfolio.html` in your deployed site
2. Click "Edit Profile" to update your name
3. Upload your athlete photo
4. Edit the About, Philosophy, and Partnership sections
5. Update your contact information
6. All changes are saved locally in your browser

## Data Storage

All data is stored locally in your browser using `localStorage`. This means:

✅ **Pros:**
- No backend needed
- Fast and instant
- Works offline
- Free hosting via GitHub Pages
- Your data stays private

⚠️ **Cons:**
- Data is device-specific (won't sync across devices)
- Clearing browser data will erase your training log
- Limited to ~5-10MB storage

### Backup Your Data

To backup your data:
1. Open browser console (F12)
2. Go to Application → Local Storage
3. Right-click and "Export" or copy the data
4. Save to a file
